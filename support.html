Support

dadsMaxxing for iPhone and Apple Watch

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. Tell us your device, its iOS or watchOS version, and what happened.

How does the program work?

Do one all-out max test. Your target becomes about 60% of your max. Each session is 5 sets at the target. Hit it on every set and the target goes up by one. A new max test is due after 9 sessions, or sooner once the target passes 80% of your max.

Why didn't I get a reminder?

  • Check that Options › Workout reminder is ON.
  • Reminders only come on days a session is due: after a full rest day, until you log one.
  • Allow notifications for dadsMaxxing in your iPhone's Settings › Notifications.
  • If you use both devices, open the iPhone app once after changing the reminder on the Watch.
  • The iPhone sends the reminder. It shows on your Watch when your iPhone is locked.

How do I remove a session logged by mistake?

In the Hall of Gains, swipe the session left and tap DELETE. Your target, max and countdown go back to what they would have been without it.

My iPhone and Watch don't match

They sync through iCloud. Make sure both are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, then open the app on each. Sync can take a minute.

How do I start over?

Options › Erase All Gains clears everything on both devices.

Does it work without an iPhone?

Yes. The Watch app runs on its own, and it schedules its own reminders when no iPhone app is installed.
