Push-ups, three times a week. For iPhone and Apple Watch.

Start with one all-out max test. Your daily target becomes about 60% of it. Do 5 sets with a rest timer between them. Hit the target on every set and it goes up by one next time. Every few weeks, a boss fight: retest your max.

What's in it

  • Log reps with the Digital Crown or on your iPhone.
  • Rest timer with a wrist tap when it's time to go.
  • A rank from LV 1 WET NOODLE to LV 5 FULL SWOLE, plus a watch-face complication.
  • Optional reminder on days a session is due.
  • Not a dad? Switch to Mom, Dude, Chick, Kid or just you.
  • Original chiptune workout music.
  • The Watch app also works without an iPhone.
