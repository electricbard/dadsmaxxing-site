Privacy Policy

dadsMaxxing for iPhone and Apple Watch · Last updated October 16, 2026

In short: dadsMaxxing doesn't collect any information about you. Your workouts stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account. The developer can't see them.

What the app stores

The app saves your workout history (dates, reps, max tests and targets) and your settings, such as sets, rest length, reminder time, music and which player you picked.

Where it's stored

  • On your devices. Your iPhone and Apple Watch each keep a copy.
  • In your iCloud account. If you're signed in to iCloud, a copy is kept in your iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This is how your iPhone and Watch stay in sync. It's private to your Apple Account and covered by Apple's privacy policy. If iCloud is off, nothing leaves your devices.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No analytics, tracking, ads or third-party code.
  • No servers. The app never sends your data to the developer or anyone else.
  • No access to Health, location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Notifications

If you turn on the workout reminder, the app schedules it on your device. No push service or server is involved.

Deleting your data

To delete one session, swipe it left in the Hall of Gains. To delete everything, use Options › Erase All Gains, which also clears the copy in iCloud. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
